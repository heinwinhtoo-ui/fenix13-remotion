# NIGHT ENGINE — film direction and edit

The campaign's visual object is a twisted copper ribbon around a small hot core.
It suggests heat and a magnetic new meeting place without literal fire, food,
mascots, or familiar studio-logo icons. Broad reflected softboxes give the copper
recognizable surfaces. Cream extended typography counterbalances its curves.

The film alternates deep black, hot orange, and one warm paper scene, using
contrast changes to reset attention. Violet remains a minor reflected accent.
Small location/type annotations are secondary; main messages carry the edit.

Motion uses smooth quartic and cubic easing, character offsets, true perspective,
depth travel, beveled letter surfaces, light-driven highlights, and geometric
occlusion across cuts. Motion is frame-addressable, not time-accumulated: seeking
to a frame produces the same pose as rendering it in sequence.

No continuous hiss, noise bed, film grain, voice, sample loops, or fire crackling.
Movement sounds use harmonically softened frequency sweeps. The strongest bass
and logo events remain short and leave headroom for phone playback.
