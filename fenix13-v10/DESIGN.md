# V10 visual identity

Warm international hospitality, detailed charcoal, amber practical lights,
ivory typography and phoenix orange. Inviting social dining, no human imagery.
The film progresses from ignition through location, drinks and food to an empty
social setting, a dedicated 3D creature sequence and a clear brand hold.

- Charcoal `#070706`: consistent backdrop.
- Ivory `#fff0d8`: headlines and final identity support.
- Phoenix orange `#ff792f`: selected emphasis, location and fire.
- Natural amber/copper: practical lights and reflective surfaces.
- Manrope 800/500: the sole on-screen copy family. Brand artwork is unchanged.

Use 84 px horizontal margins for main copy. Masked line reveals last 0.7 seconds
with 0.13-second line offset; no stretch, spins, bounce or glitch. No decorative
microcopy on the final card. Photographic scene transitions use brief warm lens
veils with limited brightness. The hero stays fully within the portrait frame.

Do not add faces, hands, people, silhouettes, neon overload, unrelated mascots,
commercial music, random slogans or unverified claims about the actual venue.
