## New 3D asset

`blender/build_phoenix.py` constructs the actual feather/body meshes, articulated
armature and authored animation. The editable `.blend`, interchange `.glb`,
model report and source script are included. This is genuine volumetric geometry
with skeletal animation, not a billboard or a moving photograph. Its visual
finish is procedural/stylized-natural, not a photorealistic studio creature.
Fire uses emissive shading, light, fragment breakup and reactive particles;
there is no fluid-simulated fire or simulated volumetric smoke cache.

## Retained geography, typography and music

Natural Earth public-domain world/Myanmar/Shan/regional-road data. Taunggyi:
20.781999 N, 97.038001 E. The pin indicates the city, not a restaurant address.
Local Manrope, Syne and Space Mono with their OFL licenses; V10 on-screen copy
uses Manrope. Brand lettering is part of the preserved prepared artwork.

The V9 music is original synthesis documented by its source. V10 reuses the
instrument-building functions and original harmonic language, rearranges the
score and synthesizes new short sizzle, air, ice and ignition cues. No commercial
song or external recorded sample is used. No continuous noise bed is added.

HyperFrames authoring/CLI skills and the legacy project were inspected. The
available HyperFrames workflow supplies HTML animation/rendering, not an
available generative-video service here. Remotion + Three.js + Blender is used
for the actual V10 composition. No unsupported HeyGen generation is claimed.
