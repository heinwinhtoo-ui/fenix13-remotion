Reviewed a contact sheet covering every second of the full 40-second V9 preview,
plus its source, assets, scene timing, phoenix rig and soundtrack synthesis.
Decoded the entire reference when generating the sheet; no unavailable original
reference footage was substituted or claimed to have been inspected.

Strongest elements retained: amber/ivory/charcoal identity; accurate globe →
Myanmar → Taunggyi concept; readable brand hold; appetizing skewer and wing
imagery; original clean musical palette; phoenix as the closing brand cue.

Weaknesses addressed: ten seconds on geographic setup; no fast charcoal hook;
repeated still-image camera movement; generated human faces in social scene;
phoenix appearing too early during GREAT NIGHTS; a deforming photo rather than
an articulated creature; typographic horizontal fitting and repeated flips;
extra microcopy around the final card; insufficient scene-specific sound cues.

The new environment shots remain plate-based compositing, but add independent
liquid/heat/vapor/light motion. They are not misrepresented as live-action or
AI-generated video. The creature is now a real animated mesh with 43 joints;
its locally authored surface finish remains stylized-natural.
