# Asset provenance

- **Phoenix, grill and lounge:** original procedural 3D geometry in
  `src/Cinema3D.tsx`. Editable mesh construction and absolute-time articulation.
  No downloaded models, footage or photographic textures. Studio reflection
  panels and engraved feather bump texture are generated locally in code.
- **Prepared logo:** `public/brand/fenix13-chill-grill.png`, carried forward from
  V5/V6. An image-generation reconstruction based on the user's inline artwork;
  not a pixel-identical original logo file. Final mark and glyphs use this same
  asset. The 3D flying sculpture is an original interpretation of that identity.
- **Maps:** Natural Earth public-domain world/country/state vectors and selected
  regional roads. Taunggyi coordinate 97.038001 E, 20.781999 N. Regional locator,
  not a street-level map or venue address. See bundled geodata for the geometry.
- **Fonts:** bundled Syne, Manrope and Space Mono, with their OFL licenses.
- **Audio:** original deterministic synthesis in `scripts/score60.py`. Music,
  beat, sound-design and motif stems are included. Warm electric-piano voicings,
  bass, percussion and tonal flight/merge cues; no third-party recordings,
  white-noise layers, crackling, speech or sampled songs.

Dependencies retain their respective package licenses. All required runtime
assets are bundled and can be rendered without external asset services.
