`npm run verify` writes `QUALITY_REPORT.json` from the actual final export:
1080×1920, 3600 frames, 60/1 fps, H.264 yuv420p, exact 60-second presentation,
stereo AAC 48 kHz, full-file FFmpeg decode, MP4 fast start, audio-master
correlation, non-clipping decoded audio and unique adjacent motion-frame hashes.

The full master is reviewed again through a timeline contact sheet and a dense
merge-transition sheet before publication. The 540×960 preview retains 60 fps
and is decoded end to end. Source includes the deterministic geometry and score.
