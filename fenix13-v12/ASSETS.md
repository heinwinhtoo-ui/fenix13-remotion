simulation. The final artwork is not redesigned or recolored. No creature model
or flying bird is included.

concept plates and local shader effects. They are not actual venue footage.

composition; typography, geometry and maps are rendered at output resolution.

coordinate remain. The location is not a venue street address. Local fonts retain

commercial song, recorded sample, voice or wing/flight sounds are used. Brief
