# FENIX /13 — Coming Soon

## Intent

Create an original, premium, typography-led nightlife advertisement for FENIX /13, a BBQ, bar and restaurant opening soon in Taunggyi, Shan State. The emotional arc moves from a single ember to heat, attitude, rhythmic energy, social anticipation, and a polished brand lockup.

## Creative direction

The film is entirely motion graphics and typography. Its visual language combines deep warm black, molten copper-orange light, restrained violet depth, geometric masks, ember particles, editorial scale, and cinematic easing. It contains no live action, photography, characters, or voiceover.

## Audio

Use an original locally synthesized electronic score with low bass impacts, filtered whooshes, ember-like noise, a rising transition, rhythmic ticks, and a final logo hit.

## Delivery

1080×1920, 30 fps, exactly 20 seconds, H.264 MP4. Preserve editable HTML/CSS/JavaScript/GSAP source and local assets.
