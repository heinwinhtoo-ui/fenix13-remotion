# Night Ignition

Version 2 of the FENIX /13 coming-soon advertisement.

Palette: ink #070708, warm cream #fff2dd, molten orange #ff6b20, copper #a84320,
copper highlights #ffd396, restrained violet #8054e8, warm muted text #b6a99c.
Colors use these exact values or alpha variants throughout.

Typography: Anton 400 for kinetic statements; Montserrat 900 for the FENIX /13
brand; Montserrat 700 for short supporting phrases; Space Mono 400 for metadata.
Every font is locally embedded with its license. Main statements sit inside
118px side margins and the 192px title-safe vertical inset.

Layers: a near-black procedural light field; copper vector slabs and sparse
embers; heavyweight type; purposeful orange rails; diagonal transition curtains.
The slash is a physical copper object formed from SVG planes, highlight edges,
and perspective. Violet appears only on background light ribbons.

Choreography varies by scene: character cascade, masked wipe, extruded perspective
landing, opposing slot change, three distinct beat hits, rhythmic stack, and brand
assembly. Final supporting text completes by 17.7s, with a readable hold until 20s.
All sound is original synthesis, on a 120 BPM subdivision grid.

Avoid photos, characters, busy particles, UI-style cards, scene timestamps, and
arbitrary palette changes. Grads are confined to localized light and copper surfaces.
