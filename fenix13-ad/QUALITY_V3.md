# Version 3 — The Arrival / validation

## Sound revision

The active timeline has exactly one audio element, pointing to
assets/v3/clean-master.wav. No earlier score or SFX appears on this timeline.

The clean score is synthesized from pitched oscillators, smoothed attacks and
musical partials. The generator contains no noise or sample sources, noisy hats,
crackle or noise risers. It includes a new original resolving logo motif.

Measured source master: -16.98 LUFS integrated, -3.65 dBTP, 4.10 LU loudness range.
Energy above 9 kHz decreased from 1.041858217% in the V2 master to approximately
0.00000123% in V3. This measurement supports the elimination of the earlier
broadband hiss; it is not a claim of subjective listening review.

## Motion revision

The final name is real beveled Three.js geometry built from Montserrat outlines,
with environment lighting, copper side faces and a subtle reflected lockup.
Overlapping font contours were unioned before extrusion to remove internal seams.
The logo camera pulls back while glyphs rotate and assemble from depth. A
deterministic three-dimensional light arc terminates on the slash.

Other scenes use optical glyph reveals, hinged letter construction, perspective
word handoffs, a clipped CSS 3D drum and folding social typography. Every seam uses
an optical shutter transition.
