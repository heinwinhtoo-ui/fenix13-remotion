# FENIX /13 — The Arrival / Version 3

## Style Prompt
A cinematic studio ident for a nightlife brand. A single living thread of copper
light becomes a sculptural slash and assembles a dimensional wordmark. The camera
travels through a coherent abstract world; typography hinges, revolves, and
reforms with deliberate weight. The final name is real beveled 3D typography,
with warm reflected light and a quiet grounded reflection.

## Colors
- Near-black #070708, the persistent canvas.
- Warm cream #fff2dd, readable front faces and text.
- Orange #ff6b20, dominant copper light.
- Copper #a84320 and gold #ffd396, metal side faces and highlights.
- Violet #8054e8, restrained reflected light only.
- Muted warm #b6a99c, secondary text.

## Typography
Anton 400 for kinetic statements. Montserrat 900 for original FENIX /13 beveled
3D lettering; Montserrat 700 for supporting copy. Space Mono 400 for small labels.
Fonts are embedded locally. Title safe: 108px sides, 192px top/bottom.

## Motion
An optical ember reveal, hinged letter construction, 3D word carousel, folding
social typography and a studio-scale 3D logo assembly. Maintain 20 seconds,
1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264. Every visual is abstract graphics or typography.

## Sound
Original clean tonal score, with warm chords, mellow plucked notes, soft sine
bass and a resolving bell phrase. No noise generators, hiss, crackle, distorted
drums, white/pink noise sweeps, or abrasive risers.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not repeat the earlier flat slide/scale entrances across every scene.
- Do not use grain overlays or busy ember fields.
- Do not copy another studio's logo, characters, castle, lamp, or theme melody.
- Do not add live action, photos, mascots, voiceover, or an invented opening date.
- Do not fade the final brand card to black; hold it readable to the end.
