# Molten Editorial

## Overview

A premium nightlife system built from oversized condensed typography, copper light, precise metadata, and sparse violet depth. The film begins nearly empty and gains density until the final lockup.

## Composition

Headlines occupy 70–90% of the safe width and anchor to opposing edges. Fine rules, coordinates, scene numbers, and a persistent vertical slash provide editorial structure. Ambient particles stay subordinate to the type.

## Constraints

- Copper-orange is the only dominant accent; violet remains atmospheric.
- Avoid rounded cards, generic gradients, centered web-layout stacks, and excessive particles.
- Maintain at least 90 px side margins and 130 px top/bottom safety.
- Supporting copy must remain readable on a phone-sized preview.
